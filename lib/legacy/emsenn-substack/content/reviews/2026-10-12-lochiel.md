- **Indigenous relations:** distinguish author’s stated Lakota position, Black Elk's mediated account recorded/edited by Joseph Epes Brown, Yaqui examples and the Tohono O'odham Nation's own position. No one person's account supplies another nation's consent.
- **Charleston/Gadsden chain:** current text properly disavows a traced transfer of slave-trade proceeds into border purchase. Preserve this evidential boundary; check dates and source anchors before public release.
- **Historical/ecological comparisons:** keep Hadley and Sheridan's ecology inference explicitly qualified. Other sites (Wood Mountain, Cananea, Río Yaqui) are comparative, not stand-ins for Lochiel.
- **Composition:** the “after Grandmother” first-person framing is the author's editorial choice, not something research operations can settle. Ensure the final piece remains authored literary prose, not a dressed-up research paper.
